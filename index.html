About

Built on Stock, Speed & Scale

Alloy Steel India Agency was founded by Nilesh Agarwal and has spent 25+ years importing and stocking alloy tool steel for mould makers and traders across India. What started as a single trading counter has grown into a three-site operation across Mumbai — a dedicated warehouse, an office with its own cutting line, and a storage yard — built specifically around one problem: buyers need the right grade, the right size, and no waiting.

We import in bulk directly, which means the grades we carry — 2311, 2738, 2738HH, H13 and 2083 — are held in depth and across a wide size range. That volume is also what lets us hold a working stock of general-purpose grades like D2, D3, WPS, EN series and MS alongside our core line. Our own in-house cutting machines turn that stock into exact-size pieces to a customer's drawing, so we can provide you with exactly what's needed.

The result, after 25 years in the trade: deep, ready stock; fast turnarounds on cut sizes; and rates that reflect what we pay on bulk import rather than what a small trader pays per piece.
