About The Family

What Is P20 Steel?

P20 is an AISI-designated chromium-molybdenum mould steel, supplied pre-hardened and tempered so it can be machined into a cavity without further heat treatment. It's the most widely specified plastic-mould steel in the world — which also means "P20" gets used loosely to mean several closely related grades rather than one exact spec.

In practice, when a customer or a drawing calls out "P20," it usually means one of three grades we stock: 2311 (standard P20 modified, our most-ordered grade), 2738 (a nickel-alloyed P20 variant with better through-hardness for large blocks), or 2738HH (2738 heat-treated to a higher hardness band for longer tool life). We also stock 2083, a stainless P20-type grade, for jobs that need corrosion resistance.

If you're not sure which of these matches your job, use the comparison below or call us — most sizing and grade questions get answered the same day.
